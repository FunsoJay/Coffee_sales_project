# Coffee Store Sales Project
![](https://github.com/FunsoJay/Coffee_sales_project/blob/main/coffee%20bkgrnd%20picture.png)

## Overview
The coffee market in New York is vibrant and fast-paced, with customers’ preferences shifting across seasons, products, and locations. For Maven Roasters, a local coffee chain with three city outlets, understanding not just what sells but when and why is key to driving sustainable growth.  

This project analyzed six months of sales data across three locations, using Power BI to highlight peak periods, best-selling products, and customer trends, delivering insights to boost revenue, optimize inventory, and align strategies with demand.

## Business Objectives
The regional store manager wants to assess sales performance over the past six months and identify areas for operational improvement. The following questions guide this analysis:
- How have Maven Roasters sales trended over time?
- Which days of the week tend to be busiest, and why do you think that's the case?
- Which products are sold most and least often sold?
- Which drive the most revenue for the business?

## Skills Demonstrated
The analysis leveraged the following Power BI features:
- Data Cleaning and Transformation(Power Query)
- DAX(Data Analysis Expressions)
- Data Visualization and Reporting

## Analysis and Insights
#### 1. Revenue Trend
- Since the start of the year, the business has enjoyed steady month-on-month revenue growth—pausing only in February with a slight dip caused by a shift in sales strategy.
**June recorded the highest revenue at $166K**, and projections show this upward momentum continuing through year-end—a clear sign that the revised business strategy is paying off.
#### 2. Busiest sales day and why?
- **Fridays** see the highest coffee orders, driven by customers winding down the week with social meetups, casual hangouts, and a well-earned treat.
- **Mondays** also see strong order volumes, as customers seek an energy boost and a fresh start to reset for the new week.

#### 3. Top Selling and Least Selling(by Product Name)
- Together, Barista Espresso, Brewed Chai Tea, Hot Chocolate, and Gourmet Brewed Coffee account for **roughly 45% of total revenue**, standing out as the best-sellers in both quantity and revenue.
- Green Beans, Green Tea, and Organic Chocolate see very low customer demand, which could result in losses if excess inventory is maintained.

#### 4. Top Revenue Driver(Product category) and Top-Selling Store
- **Coffee and Tea** generate 67% of total revenue (**$698K**), highlighting their strong customer appeal and consistent demand.
- **Hell’s Kitchen leads** in revenue, though by close margins—showing that all three stores are performing profitably.


## Recommendations
1. With revenue growing, the business should focus on loyalty programs and customer accounts to strengthen relationships, boost repeat purchases, and drive retention.
2. Opening new stores would expand the business’s reach, attract more customers, and drive further growth, especially since surveys indicate strong demand for outlets closer to customers.
3. Offering discounted prices especially for top-selling products at select times can encourage higher purchases and boost referrals, driving both sales and customer growth.
